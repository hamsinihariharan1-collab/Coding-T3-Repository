Go Phish

Game Description

Go Phish is a phishing simulation tool that allows players to test their email reading skills to find malicous intent in suspicous looking emails. Test your critical thinking and cybersecurity skills as you work to protect your company from viruses, and future problems. Can you successfully defend the organisation and keep your job, or will one wrong click lead to disaster?

Educational Content

Go Phish is an educational game designed to teach players about cybersecurity. Through analysing emails and identifying potential threats, players learn how to recognise common phishing scams, suspicious links, and malicious attachments. The game encourages critical thinking and attention to detail, by requiring players to examine email content before making decisions. Players are exposed to realistic examples of both legitimate and fraudulent communications, helping them understand how cybercriminals attempt to deceive users. By playing the game, users develop practical skills that can be applied in real life situations, such as identifying warning signs of phishing attacks, protecting personal information, and making informed decisions when interacting with digital communications. The credit based system reinforces the consequences of cybersecurity mistakes while rewarding careful analysis and sound judgement, creating an engaging and interactive learning experience.
