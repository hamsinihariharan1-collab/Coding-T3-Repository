Big T's Cyberforest

You find yourself at the entrance of the Cyberforest, surrounded by towering oak trees and the darkness of the night sky above. Within the depths of the Cyberforest, you discover a lost phone. You must use the phone to shoot hackers before they have a chance of infecting your phone with a virus. At the very end of the Cyberforest, the hacker leader known as Haszalegend awaits you. Remember to close any pop-ups and to think twice before clicking a suspicious link. Good luck, and don’t get hacked…
