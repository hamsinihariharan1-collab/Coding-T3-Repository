Overview

Big T's Cyberforest is a short, educational game that promotes cybersecurity and raises awareness of malicious hackers online.

While playing this game, you navigate through the Cyberforest while interacting with a lost phone. You can use the phone to shoot hackers that block your path. You must defeat the hacker boss, Haszalegend, and return the phone back to Big T.

Educational Benefits

By playing Big T's Cyberforest, you will be able to learn how to protect yourself against malicious threats such as viruses and hackers. You will be able to use the phone in the game to run diagnostics if your device gets hacked.

While playing the game, you will encounter scam emails and pop-up advertisements, helping you identify obvious scam attempts while spreading awareness of online scams in general.

Big T's Cyberforest also emphasises the importance of keeping electronic devices and technology safe, as you are required to return a lost phone back to its rightful owner. The game also encourages you to use electronic devices in a safe manner.
